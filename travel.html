Travel

Welcome to the Big Apple!

Accommodations

There are lots of housing options in the NYC area. We recommend looking for hotels or Airbnbs in Manhattan, Brooklyn, or Jersey City.

Here are some considerations that can help you decide where to stay:

  • How long is my stay? Do I want to explore multiple neighborhoods in the NYC area while I’m there?
  • How close is the hotel to the nearest public transportation?
  • How close is the hotel to the airport I’m flying into?

Flights

There are 3 airports in the NYC area: John F Kennedy International Airport, LaGuardia Airport, and Newark Liberty International Airport.

We recommend flying into JFK because it has many flight options, has direct public transit connection into the city, and is closest to the venue.

Transportation

NYC has a robust public transportation system that we encourage you to use! The subways and buses don’t require a special card for you to ride, you can simply use tap-to-pay.

We also don’t recommend you to rent a car—traffic can be very congested, and parking can be expensive and difficult to find. To drive around, you can call an Uber/Lyft or taxi.
