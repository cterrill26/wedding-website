Frequently Asked Questions

You asked, we answered.

  • Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

    The ceremony and cocktail hour will be outdoors (weather permitting) and the reception will be indoors.

  • Is there parking at the venue?

    Yes, there is a parking lot at the venue for guests who wish to drive themselves.

  • What time should I arrive at the venue?

    Please arrive at the Brooklyn Botanic Garden at 6PM. The ceremony begins at 6:30PM.

  • Is there a dress code?

    The dress code is formal. We encourage breathable fabrics and shoes suitable for grass. Bonus points for light spring colors and floral patterns—here’s some inspiration if you need it!

  • Can I bring a date?

    Please check your invitation for plus ones!

  • Are kids allowed?

    We are hoping to create a relaxed, adult-friendly environment for the evening, though we are leaving the option open for you to bring your little ones.

  • Do you have a registry?

    No! Your presence at our wedding is the greatest gift we could ask for. We are truly thankful for the love from our friends and family who are able to celebrate our big day with us.

Special thank you to Raya Studios for taking our engagement photos and to Raven Mo for our custom lettered logo and monogram!
