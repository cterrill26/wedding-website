The Venue

The Palm House, 1000 Washington Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11225

The ceremony will take place outdoors in the garden, followed by cocktail hour by the lily ponds. Dinner and dancing will be held indoors in the Palm House.

The Brooklyn Botanic Garden is easily accessible by car or NYC public transit. Parking is available onsite for guests.

We will have a shuttle bus at the Hampton Inn Downtown/Brooklyn (where our hotel block is) that will transport guests to and from the venue. Guests are welcome to take the shuttle bus even if they’re not staying in our hotel block.
